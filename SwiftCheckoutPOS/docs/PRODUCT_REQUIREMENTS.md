## 1\. Product and fixed scope

SwiftCheckoutPOS is a standalone offline-first POS on **one Windows computer**, locally hosted with the existing **ASP.NET Core MVC/.NET 10** solution, **local SQL Server**, locally connected POS hardware and local records/configuration/logging/backups. Internet is not required for any core function. It must preserve committed business records and recoverable active/held work across process/Windows restarts and approved upgrades.

Retain MVC/UI/BLL/DAL and existing features. Multi-terminal/network databases, branches, cloud hosting/storage/identity, synchronization, remote access, microservices and message brokers are outside scope. No SQLite migration or architecture rebuild is proposed.

The actual source/audit baseline, SQL edition evaluation and target architecture are in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); detailed evidence/VERIFIED-CONFIRMED-SUSPECTED findings remain in [PROJECT\_AUDIT.md](PROJECT_AUDIT.md). No current feature is certified Works by this requirements document. Checkout was restored and built in the earlier implementation task; schema/integration and remaining audit gates are still open.

## 5. Approved business rules

The owner-confirmed 31-10-2026 version of [OWNER_DECISIONS.md](OWNER_DECISIONS.md) is the source of truth. Section 6 records all 93 statuses. A deliberate device-model deferral is an approved answer, not an invented model; only PR-Q63 is explicitly PENDING. Approval of an answer does not silently resolve the cross-rule questions in section 7.

### A. Money and rounding

BDT/paisa explicitly, independent of Windows regional settings. Cost/retail/rates retain two decimal places; never whole-round stored prices during edit/display. Round each calculated line to two decimals, aggregate bill VAT to two, final total to two; midpoint away from zero. Cash final payable then rounds to whole taka, with stored/printed delta; Card/bKash/Nagad exact. Mixed cash splits need CQ-01. Sources: PR-Q01–07, PR-Q91.

### B. VAT / tax

Prices are VAT-inclusive. The shop is **not VAT-registered now**: all products 0%; receipts show no VAT line and no BIN. Only Admin controls the retained per-product VAT rates for future registration, at which time PR-Q10 must be updated. No automatic jurisdiction rules. Allocate bill discount proportionally to item values before extracting VAT. Preserve historic tax facts; do not rewrite prior sales to zero. Sources: PR-Q08–10.

### C. Discounts

Cashier/Manager/Admin may discount; item discounts are required. One bill discount mode only; reject both flat and percentage. Limits cover item + bill discounts combined: Cashier ≤25%, Manager ≤50%, Admin no role cap. Cashier above limit needs Manager/Admin; Manager above limit needs Admin. Every discount records giver/approval. Net item below cost is blocked unless Manager/Admin approves with a reason; Cashier sees “Discount too high — ask Manager,” never cost. Sources: PR-Q11–13, PR-Q67, PR-Q90, PR-Q93; calculation basis and Manager-OFF overrides need CQ-02/03.

### D. Stock and held orders

Reject shortages/negative availability. Held units are unavailable to others but remain owned stock for value/WAC. Cancellation releases reservation, keeps Cancelled history; expire after configured 72h, with Manager/Admin setting, 0=never. Moving WAC includes paid batch cost (transport optional in staff-entered total), and held quantity. Free/bonus restock may cost zero only with reason and no cash ledger entry. Default below-cost price blocked; Manager/Admin reasoned override retained. Manager/Admin alone correct stock with required Count correction/Damaged/Expired/Theft-Loss/Found/Other-with-note, actor/time; decreases expense and increases gain at current average cost. Vendor credit without cash reduces quantity/value and records pending-credit note, then cash only when received. Inactive cart products block completion. Sources: PR-Q14–25, PR-Q77, PR-Q82.

### E. Refunds

Completed receipt within seven days; Manager/Admin approval; cumulative refunds never exceed sold quantity. Full-sale-only may ship in v1; item/quantity partial refunds are the next enhancement. Original tender; cash payout from drawer. Per item choose Return to stock (default) or Damaged. Proportional original VAT reversal; restocked goods reverse COGS at original cost, damaged goods have Damaged expense. Separate refund occurrence on refund date; original sale never changed/moved; linked payout/VAT/COGS facts. Damaged accounting and split/due payout allocation need CQ-05/06. Sources: PR-Q26–31, PR-Q65/66.

### F. Customers and loyalty

Link customers; no earning/redemption for now, so no points reversal until an earning/redemption rule exists. Due eligibility is separate. Sources: PR-Q32, PR-Q70/71.

### G. Finance and reports

Manual inflow: Capital Investment, Loan Received, Other Income. Manual outflow: Rent, Utilities, Salaries, Transport, Maintenance, Other Expense, Capital Withdrawal, Loan Repayment. Inventory/COGS/Inventory Adjustment/Damaged are system-only. Net Cash Position = (sales collected + all ledger inflows) − (refunds paid + all ledger outflows); the cash/noncash/double-counting ambiguity is CQ-07, not silently corrected here. Only the six operating expense categories count as Opex; capital/loan movements do not. Revenue/profit exclude VAT; historical profit uses known sale-time cost; unknown costs display “cost unknown” and are excluded with note. Rename “Capital payback estimate”; start at earlier first capital/sale, months=max(elapsed days/30.44,1). KPI date range only, search table only; lifetime labelled All time and ignores date filter. Business day midnight-to-midnight Asia/Dhaka (UTC+6). Sources: PR-Q33–40, PR-Q58/59, PR-Q79–81.

### H. Till and cash drawer

One explicitly opened shift at a time; cashier enters float. Expected cash = float + shift cash sales − shift cash refunds + paid in − paid out. Blind count first; Manager/Admin see expected/count afterwards. Record discrepancy, counted-by and note; >BDT1000 approval, no shortage expense posting. Drawer only cash sale/refund; manual No sale needs Manager/Admin reason/audit. Sources: PR-Q41–44, PR-Q47, PR-Q88; negative threshold/out-of-shift handling need CQ-08/12.

### I. Receipts and payments

Assign SCP-YYYYMMDD-0001 only at successful completion, sequential per Dhaka day and never reuse. Separate terminal/phone payment confirmed manually; optional last-four/bKash/Nagad reference, no payment integration. Admin-only shop name/address/phone/BIN (only registered)/footer settings. Receipt contains saved sale date/cashier/items/discount/conditional VAT/rounding/total/tenders/paid/change; never cost/profit. Cashier/Manager may reprint: REPRINT / COPY, current reprint time/actor logged, original values retained. Sources: PR-Q45/46/48/83.

### J. Staff, login and permissions

PR-Q50/92 matrix in section 2; Admin includes Manager operations. Anyone logged in may resume a hold, recording both holder/resumer. Lock after 15 minutes idle without discarding cart; same user or Manager/Admin password unlock; ticket ≤12h. Owner creates initial Admin once during local install, no default password; Admin resets others, owner-only emergency local recovery. Benchmark current recommended hashing under one second on shop PC; version rehash format. Five failed logins cause 30s increasing up to five minutes, no permanent lockout; log failures. Ramp details remain CQ-09. Sources: PR-Q49–52, PR-Q62/64/68/78.

### K. Hardware and media

Printer/scanner/drawer models deliberately deferred. Expected 80mm ESC/POS USB/virtual-COM, clearly labelled Virtual until qualification; scanner HID keyboard wedge, no serial requirement; RJ11 drawer via printer pulse. Barcode letters/digits/hyphen, 4–32, no spaces/symbols. Images JPG/PNG/WebP, ≤2MB and ≤2000×2000 pixels. Sources: PR-Q60/61, PR-Q72–76.

### L. Backup and recovery

Daily 23:00 Asia/Dhaka plus missed-run catch-up; manual pre-upgrade backup. RPO at most one business day; RTO selling within two hours. Retain 30 daily + first backup of each month for 12 months, never newest verified deletion. Daily USB copy away from PC. Preserve images/settings; virtual receipt text not required because saved-sale reprint must exist. Restore drill monthly and pre-upgrade. Capacity estimate PR-Q63 PENDING; no volume invented or record purge. The single procedure remains [DATA_RECOVERY_PLAN.md](DATA_RECOVERY_PLAN.md). Sources: PR-Q53–57, PR-Q63/69/84/89.

### M. Additional decisions

Up to two tender allocations from Cash/Card/bKash/Nagad/Due, stored separately. Registered customers only for Due, max unpaid BDT2500, Manager/Admin approval per due sale and recorded balances/collections. Manager/Admin may void within five minutes with reason, restore stock and keep Voided sale; afterwards refund. Financial privacy: Admin-only by default; Admin-only audited AllowManagerFinancialView default false permits Manager financial **read**. Protected fields are all profit/margin/cost/average/value/COGS, period revenue/sales totals/Z-report/dashboard, ledger/capital/loans/NCP/payback/customer profit. Manager OFF retains operational inventory with no cost/margin/value/sort, batch-paid input only; generic below-cost messages; six-category expenses with own entries today only; one-shift expected/count only; one-refund receipt only. No protected fields sent to browser. Backup/restore/download Admin-only regardless toggle. Sources: PR-Q85–93; unresolved interactions are listed below.
